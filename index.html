Arash Akbari

PhD Student at Northeastern University · akbari.ara@northeastern.edu

I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Engineering at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Yanzhi Wang. My research focuses on efficient deep learning and foundation models. I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Tehran.

Previously, I interned at CNRS with Timothée Masquelier and at CISPA with Xiao Zhang.

Research focus. I develop efficient machine learning techniques to make state-of-the-art models more practical for real-world deployment. My work centers on optimizing foundation models, such as Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, for scalability and deployment efficiency across diverse hardware platforms.

Feel free to reach out via email if you’d like to discuss research or potential collaborations.
