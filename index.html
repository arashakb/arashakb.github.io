Arash Akbari

PhD Student at Northeastern University · akbari.ara@northeastern.edu

Seaport District, Boston, MA

I am a PhD student in Computer Engineering at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Yanzhi Wang. I work on vision-language-action (VLA) models and world action models, with a focus on making physical AI more efficient and practical to deploy in the real world.

I earned my M.S. in Computer Engineering from Northeastern University and my B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Tehran. I have also been a research intern at CNRS with Timothée Masquelier and at CISPA with Xiao Zhang.

Feel free to reach out via email if you’d like to discuss research or potential collaborations.
